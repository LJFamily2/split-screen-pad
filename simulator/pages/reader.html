Why split screen matters on an 8.7" tablet

4 min read

A small tablet is big enough for two things at once, but only if the split is adjustable. A fixed 50:50 wastes half the screen when one side is a video and the other is a set of notes.

The controls people already expect come from three places: One UI contributed the tap-the-divider menu, app pairs and the three-app layout, HyperOS contributed drag-to-the-edge full screen, and iPadOS contributed the dock you pull apps from.

What matters more than any single feature is that the state survives. Close the app, come back, and the same two things should still be there at the same ratio.
